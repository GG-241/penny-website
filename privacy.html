4 Data that stays on your device

These are processed on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Everything, if you don't sign in. Your data lives in a local database on the device.
  • Receipt photos. Text is recognised on-device (Google ML Kit, offline). The images are not uploaded.
  • Voice input. Speech is converted to text by your phone's built-in speech recognition. Depending on your device settings, Apple or Google may process the audio under their own privacy terms. Smart Penny does not store or upload recordings.
  • Error reports. Kept in a short in-memory log on the device. They leave the device only if you choose to share one with us.
  • Biometric unlock. Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint checks are done by the operating system. We never receive biometric data.

8 Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Every table is protected by row-level security, so an account can only reach its own records and the records shared with it.
  • Sign-in tokens and encryption keys are kept in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore).
  • Optional end-to-end encryption of synced transaction details (AES-256-GCM, off by default, turn on in Settings). With it on, the key stays on your devices and we cannot read those details.
  • Shared payloads (splits, settlements, invites) are signed with per-device keys so they can't be forged.

9 Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your data and get a copy of it (Art. 15)
  • Rectify inaccurate data (Art. 16). Most data can be edited directly in the app.
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten", Art. 17). See the next section.
  • Restrict processing (Art. 18)
  • Data portability (Art. 20): Settings -> Export / Import gives you CSV, Excel or a full JSON backup.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
  • Withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3))

To exercise any right, email itsggkoumas@gmail.com. We answer within one month (Art. 12(3)) and may ask you to confirm the request from the email address linked to your account. There is no charge.

10 Deleting your data

  • Account and all cloud data: Settings -> Account -> Delete Account. This deletes your profile, synced records, device tokens, social links, profile photo and the workspaces you own, and it happens immediately. In split groups that other people are part of, their shared ledger remains and your entry is shown as "Deleted user".
  • Only this device: Settings -> Delete All Data -> This device only, or uninstall the app.
  • Without the app: follow these steps.

11 Children

Smart Penny is not intended for children under 16 and we do not knowingly process their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12 No advertising, profiling or sale

Smart Penny contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking, and does not sell, rent or broker personal data. Spending insights are calculated for you only. They are informational and have no legal or similarly significant effect on you (no automated decision-making under Art. 22).
