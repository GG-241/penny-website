Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes — everything in the app today is free: no account, no ads, no subscription, no trial that expires. A future version may add optional paid extras, but they'll be clearly marked and optional — never a surprise paywall over features you already use.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works on first launch without signing in. An account (Sign in with Apple or Google) is only needed if you choose to turn on cloud sync or share with other people.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, by default. Nothing is uploaded unless you opt into sync or backup — and receipts are scanned on-device, so images never leave your phone. You can export or wipe everything at any time from Settings.

Can I move my data from another app?

Yes — import in minutes from YNAB, Splitwise, Revolut, N26 or Monzo, or any CSV/Excel file, with duplicate detection. Leaving is just as easy: export to CSV, Excel, PDF or a full JSON backup whenever you like. Your data is never locked in.

What if I lose my phone?

Turn on optional encrypted sync or cloud backup, or keep an exported JSON backup somewhere safe. Restore onto a new phone in minutes from Settings → Export / Import.
