Under the hood

How it works

The technical tour — for people who like to know what an app does before they install it.

1 Local-first storage

Every feature — tracking, budgets, goals, analytics, splitting — runs against a local database on your phone. No network request is required to open the app, log a transaction, or read your history. Sensitive values (biometric-gated keys, sync credentials) live in the platform's protected storage: Keychain/Secure Enclave on iPhone, Keystore on Android.

Deletions are soft by default and fully auditable, so an accidental swipe is recoverable — until you explicitly wipe data, at which point it is gone, on device and in any cloud copy.

2 Forecasting, on-device

The analytics tab projects next month from your own history — exponential smoothing (Holt-Winters) for seasonal spending patterns, plus a BG/NBD-style model for spending frequency. What-if sliders re-run the projection locally. None of it leaves the phone, and none of it needs an account.

3 Smart auto-categories

A rule engine matching on EXACT, CONTAINS, STARTS_WITH and REGEX patterns auto-categorizes new transactions, and learns from your corrections — up to 500 self-learned rules, stored locally. It's deterministic: the same input always produces the same category, and you can inspect and edit every rule.

4 Sharing and splitting without a server in the way

Transactions and settle-ups move between phones as ECDSA-signed QR codes or deep links, so recipients can verify what they import — offline, with no intermediary scanning your finances. Group splits support equal, custom-share and percentage division with per-person running balances.

5 Your data is portable

  • Import: YNAB, Splitwise, Revolut, N26 and Monzo formats, plus any CSV/Excel file — with duplicate detection.
  • Export: CSV, Excel and PDF reports, or a full JSON backup of all 23 tables.
  • Multi-currency: 15+ currencies with official EC InforEuro rates, cached offline for travel days.
  • No lock-in: if you leave, you take everything with you in open formats.

6 The optional connected layer

Sign-in (Apple or Google), cloud sync and backup, and contacts exist for people who want them. They are strictly opt-in: a fresh install makes no account-related connections, and release builds only call the services backing features you actually use. Receipt scanning OCR runs on-device — images are never uploaded.

7 Accessibility is a feature

Five text-size presets applied app-wide, a high-contrast theme that activates from the system setting, an in-app reduce-motion toggle, and colorblind-friendly directional icons on every amount — on both platforms.
