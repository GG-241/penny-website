Delete your account and data

How to permanently delete your Smart Penny account and the data associated with it.

No account? If you never signed in, all your data lives only on your device. Deleting the app, or using Settings -> Delete All Data -> This device only, removes it completely.

1 Delete from the app

  1. Open Smart Penny and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings -> Account -> Delete Account.
  3. Confirm with Delete forever.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

2 Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email SUPPORT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER from the address linked to your Smart Penny account (your Apple or Google sign-in email) with the subject Delete my account. We verify the request and delete the account within 30 days, then confirm by email.

3 What is deleted

  • Your account and profile (name, email, profile photo, sign-in identifiers)
  • All data synced to Smart Penny's servers: transactions, budgets, goals, categories, workspaces, split groups and contact matches
  • Push-notification tokens and sharing identifiers
  • Local data on the device you delete from

4 What may be kept

Items you shared with other people (for example a split-group expense) remain visible to them as part of their own records. Copies in our hosting provider's routine database backups expire on that provider's backup schedule. We keep nothing else, except where retention is required by law.
